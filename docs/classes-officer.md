# Imperial Officer: ability tree draft v0.1

Status: **draft for review**, companion to `classes.md` and `classes-sororitas.md`. Same hard-choice rules.

The Officer is the third promotion path: a Scout or Novice who stays human. Ordinary stats, scale 0.85,
lasgun or bolt pistol plus power sword. Bringing an Officer means not bringing a Marine, which is the
squad-select version of a hard choice. The class exists to make the Imperial Guard matter on the map and to
give the roster a mixed-gender human line.

Hard-choice check: Command is the Marine-squad pick (action economy for your best units), Commissariat is the
Guard-and-haven pick (useless if no Guard are present, so it is a choice about *what missions you take*),
Tactician is the solo-value pick that does not depend on who is around you.

## Interactions

- **Haven defence / retaliation missions:** the Officer is the star. Guard militia stop breaking, Summary Execution turns a rout into a line.
- **With Marines and Sisters:** Commissariat does little (they do not panic), so an Officer in an all-Astartes squad is a Command or Tactician build. That is intended.
- **Haven advisor (Phase 4):** a stationed Officer applies Fear of the Commissar region-wide and earns service toward Commissar without missions.

## Open questions

1. Summary Execution on a Marine or Sister is impossible (they do not panic). Should it work on a *corrupted* Marine as a mercy kill that stops the Chaos champion from returning (§8)? Recommendation: yes, at rank 5+.
2. Should the Officer be able to wear carapace armor only (no power armor), making the squad-select choice sharper?
3. Is Orbital Lance stepping on the Chapter Master's relic ability? Recommendation: Chapter Master gets the full orbital bombardment; Officer gets a single-tile lance.
