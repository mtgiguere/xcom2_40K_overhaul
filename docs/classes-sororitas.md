# Adepta Sororitas classes: ability tree draft v0.1

Status: **draft for review**, companion to `classes.md` (same hard-choice rules). Sisters have **no psi**.
Their shared mechanic is **Faith**: a per-mission pool (like Templar Focus) that builds when a Sister is
wounded, kills a heretic, psyker or daemon, or an ally falls nearby. **Acts of Faith** spend it. Faith keys
off **Will**, so it also feeds the corruption system from the other side: high-Will Sisters are the Chapter's
anti-Chaos insurance.

## Battle Sister (bolter + bolt pistol or chainsword)

Columns: **Zealot** (bolter and fury), **Choir** (squad faith), **Shield of Faith** (durability, anti-psyker).

| Rank | Zealot | Choir | Shield of Faith |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Righteous Fury (new)**: +crit vs heretics (Chaos), psykers and daemons. | **Hymn of Battle (new)**: adjacent allies +aim this turn; costs an action. | **Shield of Faith (new)**: flat resistance to psionic attacks; immune to Mind Control at rank 5+. |
| 2 | `Deadeye` re-skin, **Aimed Shot**. | **Act of Faith: Divine Guidance (new)**: spend Faith, an ally's next shot cannot miss. | `Fortress` re-skin, **Armour of Contempt**: immune to fire and poison tiles. |
| 3 | **Purge the Heretic (new)**: a kill on a Chaos or psyker unit refunds the action. | **War Hymn (new)**: 6-tile aura, allies gain +Will and shed panic. Cooldown. | **Martyrdom (new)**: when wounded, adjacent allies gain Faith. |
| 4 | `RapidFire`. | **Act of Faith: Spirit of the Martyr (new)**: spend Faith, an ally at 0 HP is stabilised instantly at range. | **Hand of the Emperor (new)**: once per mission, negate one hit entirely. |
| 5 | **Burn the Witch (new)**: damage vs psykers doubled; they lose their next power. | **Laud Hailer (new)**: all allies in sight gain 1 Faith. | **Light of the Emperor (new)**: aura, enemy psykers within 6 tiles have half chance to use powers. |
| 6 | **Vengeance (new)**: when an ally falls in sight, free shot at the killer. | **Act of Faith: Divine Intervention (new)**: spend all Faith, revive a fallen Sister this turn at 1 HP. 1 charge. | **Unbreakable (new)**: immune to panic, disorient, stun. |

Hard-choice check: Zealot is anti-Chaos and anti-Eldar, Choir is the squad engine, Shield of Faith is the psyker
counter. Against Orks and Tyranids, Zealot and Shield lose half their text, which is the point: the Sister built
for Chaos is not the Sister you want on a Tyranid world.

build. Martyr wants her dead at the right moment; Redemption wants her alive to graduate.

## Cross-roster synergies

- Hospitaller **Castigation** + Chaplain = the corruption cure loop.
- Battle Sister **Shield of Faith** column + Librarian = the only squad that can take a Chaos sorcerer head-on.
- Retributor **Vaporise** on Tyranid worlds = no Genestealer spawns from corpses.
- Faith rises when Marines fall nearby: a mixed squad makes Sisters stronger exactly when things go wrong.

## Open balance questions (Sisters)

1. Should Faith be per-mission (Templar Focus style) or persist between missions as a resource? Recommendation: per-mission.
2. Repentia rank 6 **Redeemed** changes class mid-career. Keep, or too odd for the promotion UI?
3. Seraphim and Assault Marine overlap heavily by design (both jump). Fine as a parallel line, or should Seraphim lean harder into pistols?
4. Art: the Sisters armor mod gives one look. Seraphim, Retributor and Repentia need borrowed parts (Jet Packs, heavy weapons, bare-armed torsos) until a larger Sororitas set is found.
